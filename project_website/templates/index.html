01 / The system

Complex enough to surprise.
Clear enough to inspect.

Every mechanic is headless, typed, and replayable. The framework exposes stable APIs while keeping content definitions easy to extend.

A

Utility decisions

Actors evaluate legal actions against health, resources, threat, statuses, cooldowns, vulnerabilities, and fading event memory.

ENGAGEDEFENDRECOVERFLEEDESPERATE
B

Body-part combat

Accuracy, critical hits, armor, damage types, durability, and localized health turn each action into a concrete consequence.

C

Procedural worlds

Terrain, biome, temperature, and precipitation layers reconstruct from versioned HDF5 chunks—without a graphics runtime.

D

Deterministic state

Seeded randomness and versioned saves make failures reproducible and encounters easy to test, share, and replay.
